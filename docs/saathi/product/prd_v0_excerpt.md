# PRD (draft for build, 16 Oct 2026) - excerpt as pasted by Priyansh in Saathi HQ, truncated at section 3.4
One-line brief: A voice agent calls the parent every morning on the phone they already own, in their language, checks they woke up, took medicines, and are not in distress, then WhatsApps a two-line briefing to the child. Missed call or reported fall -> escalate to child AND a neighbour who can physically go over. Parent never installs an app. The child is the buyer. Name deferred ("the product"/"the agent").
Why: Bolna buildathon (PSTN voice demo), YC aging RFS (voice conversation, independence monitoring, caregiver coordination). India: caregiver is adult child in another city/country; parent's interface is a ringing phone; WhatsApp is the family coordination layer; Hindi/regional languages first.
Research cited in PRD (from published sources, cite as research): 156.7M 60+ in 2024 (WHO SEARO); 15.5M 80+; 193M by 2030 (UNFPA 2025); 347M by 2050; 6% elderly live alone, 9% of elderly women (NITI/LASI); alone-or-with-spouse 22%->27% 2011->2017-18; ~36% have a migrant child; ~66% rural, ~85% no/primary education (English UI and QR onboarding will fail); 58% of elderly are women, 54% of elderly women widows; multimorbidity 32%; medication intake 81% diabetes / 73% hypertension; living alone OR 1.63 uncontrolled BP; 13.4% frequent loneliness; ~20% ADL, ~44% IADL limitation. Daily calling is the existing care practice (Ahlin ethnography); many 65+ prefer a normal incoming voice call.
