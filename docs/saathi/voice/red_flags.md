# Red flags

Companionship framing. A flag is “tell the family now”, not a diagnosis.

## EMERGENCY — alert child + neighbour immediately

- Fall / slipped / cannot get up
- Chest pain, pressure, “dil dard”
- Severe breathlessness
- Sudden confusion, not recognising the caller
- Talk of wanting to die or self-harm (escalate; do not counsel)

## URGENT — Needs a look (child); may become Act now if they sound worse

- Dizziness / chakkar with inability to walk
- High fever, vomiting, not eating
- New weakness on one side

## ATTENTION — Needs a look

- Low mood / “mann bhari”, crying
- Skipped a due tablet
- Poor sleep several days (memory note + briefing)

## Missed call

Not a medical flag. Starts Needs a look. Becomes Act now when the ladder hits neighbour + child.

## Tool

`raise_alert` with `category`: `EMERGENCY` | `URGENT` | `ATTENTION`, plus `quote` in their words, `late` only for the extraction backstop.
