# Brag Plan: Saathi

## What is this app?
Saathi is a morning phone call for an Indian parent, on the phone they already own. The child gets a short WhatsApp briefing. Companionship and reminders — never diagnosis.

## The angle
A quiet product film. The joke is that there is no new gadget: she already has a phone. The film shows the morning itself — the call, the desk, the two lines that arrive after.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“No app for her.” Then the site’s own line, large: “A morning call for Maa, on the phone she has.”

## Key moments (the middle)
- The home-screen call card: “8:00 AM · Saathi is calling” and “नमस्ते माँ, दवाई ले ली?”
- The EXAMPLE Today desk: “Maa is doing well today”, Amlodipine taken, Metformin not asked yet.
- The real daily briefing shape: “Saathi called Kamla this morning (08:30 IST). Status: Good. Medicines: Amlodipine taken.”

## Outro / punchline
The wordmark, then “She picks up and talks.” and “Not an emergency service · 112.”

## User flow worth showing
Morning call arrives on the phone she has → the family desk shows today’s medicines → the child gets the WhatsApp briefing.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: warm morning product film, paper and terracotta, no startup swagger
- Interpretation: four holds, soft motion, type that stays long enough to read, music under rather than over

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21.28

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (trimmed to the film as bed.mp3)
- Music treatment: start with the picture, volume about 0.32, no fade gimmick, hold under the logo
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset, tempo 109.96 BPM. Strong cues used: 8.74s (Today), 13.11s (briefing), 17.47s (wordmark). Beat grid for medicine rows and briefing lines.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; paper warmth and the phone’s shadow breathe with RMS. No waveform.
- SFX posture: sparse, soft, one hit per scene change
- Audio-coupled moments: call card, medicine rows one by one, briefing lines one by one, logo
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no risers, no voiceover
